These notes develop spontaneous symmetry breaking as a common organizing idea across ordered matter and field theory. The emphasis is on the relationship between symmetry, degeneracy, order parameters, and the low-energy modes that follow.

## What the notes cover

- Symmetric laws and symmetry-breaking states
- Order parameters and degenerate ground states
- Goldstone modes associated with continuous broken symmetries
- The importance of dimensionality and fluctuations
- Examples drawn from statistical mechanics and field theory

The goal is to make the shared structure behind apparently different symmetry-breaking problems easier to recognize.
