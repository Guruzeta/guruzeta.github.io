The Cahn–Hilliard equation is a minimal model for phase separation when the order parameter is conserved. These notes connect its thermodynamic construction to the patterns and growing length scales seen during coarsening.

## What the notes cover

- Free-energy functionals for a conserved order parameter
- Chemical potential and the Cahn–Hilliard equation
- Linear instability of a homogeneous state
- Domain growth, coarsening, and scaling

The presentation is meant to bridge the formal derivation and the physical picture of domains forming and merging over time.
